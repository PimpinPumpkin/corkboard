Corkboard is a native client for craigslist with Material 3, light and dark themes, and every filter each category has: make and model, year, odometer and title status for cars; bedrooms, pets and laundry for housing.

Favorites and saved searches stay on the phone. Saved-search alerts are checked by the phone itself every few hours, with no push service and no account.

The app talks to craigslist directly from your phone. There is no Corkboard server, no analytics and no Google services. Replying, posting and account pages open in your browser.

Corkboard is an independent project and is not affiliated with craigslist.
